October 31st • 11:59 PM

Everyone wore a mask. Nobody told the truth.

BREAKING NEWS

A mysterious guest has vanished from the Midnight Ball

THE STRANGER IN SILVER

At exactly midnight, a stranger dressed in silver vanished from the ballroom. Three guests claim they saw the stranger enter a locked room. The room was empty when the doors opened.

Witnesses describe the stranger as tall and unusually quiet, wearing a silver mask that concealed their entire face. One guest insists they saw a faint reflection in the ballroom mirror moments after the stranger disappeared, although nobody was standing before it. The guards have searched every corridor, yet no trace of the mysterious visitor has been found.

More updates as the mystery unfolds...

WHO LEFT THE RED ROSE?

A single red rose was discovered beside the ballroom's locked entrance. No guest has admitted to leaving it there.

The flower was found shortly after the final dance, resting neatly against the door as though someone had placed it there deliberately. Curiously, the rose showed no signs of wilting, despite the cold air that swept through the empty hallway. A few guests believe it may have been a secret message intended for the stranger in silver.

Perhaps the flower belongs to someone who has not yet revealed themselves.

THE CLOCK THAT STOPPED

Guests report that the grand clock stopped at exactly 11:59 PM. Despite several attempts to restart it, the hands refuse to move. Some say it has been this way ever since the stranger in silver arrived.

The grand clock has stood in the ballroom for over a century and has never malfunctioned before. Despite several attempts to restart it, the hands remain frozen at 11:59 PM. Guests recall the room falling strangely silent just before it stopped, while the candles flickered without a breeze. Some now wonder if the clock is connected to the stranger's mysterious disappearance.

Coincidence, or a warning?

VOICES FROM THE BALL

Some guests have agreed to share their accounts of the night's strange events.

“Oh, it was dreadful! They were right there beside the clock, and then—poof! Gone! And don't tell me it was a trick. I've attended these balls for twenty years!”

— Lady Evelyn

“Between you and me, that silver stranger was awfully suspicious. And the rose? Well, I heard someone found it by the locked door. Wouldn't you like to know who left it?”

— Mr. Finch

“A vanished guest, a stopped clock, a mysterious rose? Hmph. Everyone's making a fuss over nothing. Though I must admit… I don't remember playing the last song.”

— The ballroom pianist

“I played till the very end, but I remember no footsteps leaving the ballroom. Perhaps the stranger was never there to begin with.”

— The evening's violinist

A WORD BEFORE MIDNIGHT

The events of tonight's Midnight Ball remain unexplained. The stranger in silver has yet to be identified, the red rose remains unclaimed, and the grand clock has not moved since 11:59 PM.

Guests are advised to report any further unusual occurrences to the Gazette

Until further notice, the mystery remains open.
